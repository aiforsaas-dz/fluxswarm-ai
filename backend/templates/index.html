What is FluxSwarm?

Type a goal and a squad of 8 specialized agents — Planner, Architect, DevOps, TDD, Reviewer, Designer, Builder, Auditor — plans, builds, tests, designs, reviews and assembles it on a live task board you can watch. Generated files land in your project workspace, ready to use. Bring your own provider key for the strongest output — or a free OpenRouter key to trial free. Each launch is still a single credit. 1 credit per launch, credits never expire, no monthly fee.

Plans & pricing

    

    
Prepaid credit packs — no monthly fee. All plans include the full squad (8 agents); higher tiers add credits and parallel execution. Prices in USD; GBP is applied by Paddle at checkout for UK customers.

    

      
How are credits used?
One launch = one project board = one credit. You buy nothing monthly — packs never expire.

      
Can I try before paying?
Yes — the Demo plan includes 5 free credits, no card required. You can also bring your own provider key (BYOK) at any tier.

      
Which AI providers are supported?
Claude, GPT, Gemini and Kimi via BYOK, plus a free OpenRouter option (GLM-5.2). Output strength follows the provider you use.

      
What does the 1-credit launch fee cover?
The squad execution itself. With BYOK you pay your provider's token rate directly; FluxSwarm still only charges the single credit.

      
Refunds?
Paddle handles payments; launches that fail before any work starts are auto-refunded in credits, and our refund policy covers unused credit packs.
